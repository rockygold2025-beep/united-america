Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I open an account?

Simply click the "Register" button at the top of the page. You'll need to provide some personal information, verify your identity, and you'll be ready to bank with United Bank of America.

❓ How do I make a transfer?

Go to the Payments section from your dashboard. Enter the recipient's username or bank details, the amount, and submit. All transfers require admin approval for security.

❓ Is my money protected?

Yes! Your eligible deposits are protected by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) up to $250,000 per person, per banking group.

❓ How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Follow the instructions to reset your password via email verification.
