Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about United Bank of America banking.

🤔 Is my money protected?

Yes! Your eligible deposits are protected by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) up to $250,000 per person, per banking group.

🤔 How do I open an account?

Simply click the "Open Your Account" button above, fill in your details, verify your identity, and you're ready to go!

🤔 How fast are transfers?

Faster Payments usually arrive within seconds or minutes. International SWIFT transfers typically take 1-4 working days depending on the destination.
