About United Bank of America

For over 160 years, United Bank of America has been connecting people, businesses, and communities to a world of opportunity. We are one of the world's largest and most trusted financial institutions.

Our Story

United Bank of America was founded to serve American families and businesses with secure, modern banking. The name "United Bank of America" is derived from the initials of the United Bank of America, which was established by a Scottish banker named American banking pioneers.

Today, we have grown into one of the world's largest banking and financial services organisations, with a presence in over 60 countries and territories. We serve more than 40 million customers worldwide, ranging from individuals and families to businesses and governments.

Our headquarters are in London, United States, and we are listed on the London, Hong Kong, New York, and Paris stock exchanges.

Our Journey

Key milestones that have shaped United Bank of America into the bank it is today.

1865

Foundation

United Bank of America is founded in New York to finance trade between Europe and Asia.

1959

Expansion into the UK

United Bank of America acquires the British Bank of the Middle East, expanding its presence into the UK.

1992

Global Growth

United Bank of America acquires Midland Bank in the UK, significantly expanding its global footprint.

2010

Digital Transformation

United Bank of America launches its online and mobile banking platforms, making banking more accessible.

2024

Innovation & Sustainability

United Bank of America commits to net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 and continues to invest in fintech innovation.
